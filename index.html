Tonies® was founded to enable children to experience storytelling in a digital age that stimulates their imagination while being educational and fun, and most importantly screen-free. Their hero product, the Toniebox®, is a magical audiobox that immerses children in rich, story-led content, in worlds they already know and love. Partnering with the likes of Disney, Julia Donaldson and Roald Dahl, the product is loved by parents and children alike.

Tonies® were seeking a long-term creative partner to support their ability to scale acquisition on paid social. Whilst performance was their core focus, finding a partner who could produce creative at scale while maintaining their brand values, tone of voice and premium core was equally important.

The challenge

Build consideration before Q4, without diluting a premium brand

Our initial objective was to help Tonies® build consideration into Q4 in order to improve acquisition efficiency and overall purchase volume on Meta, YouTube and TikTok. The hypothesis was that by improving brand salience ahead of their key acquisition period, they could reach new levels of scale across their acquisition campaigns.

Our role was therefore to support the production of a large volume of on-brand assets, with a variety of creative executions, that focused on educating prospective customers on the benefits of the Toniebox® and ensuring the core product was understood.

Brand-led creative. Polished statics and product-first video that had to feel unmistakably Tonies®.Meta · YouTube
The approach

Polished brand creative, plus creators who sound like parents

We decided that a mixture of polished, brand-led creative and authentic creator ads (CGC) was the best way to achieve our objectives. Five things shaped the work.

  1. Strategic direction

    Our content had to speak to our audience: parents. We dissected core buying angles and pain points for the market and used this to guide all asset production, ensuring we covered every base.

  2. Asset volume

    The key to scale is in asset volume. The more we test, the more likely we are to find winners. We produced 300+ pieces of creative across multiple angles to amplify testing and improve the likelihood of success.

  3. Channel-specific assets

    Rather than painting every canvas with the same brush, we created bespoke assets for each core channel: Meta, YouTube and TikTok. Optimising for specific channels and placements improved performance across the board.

  4. Authentic execution

    Parents value the opinion of other parents when making purchase decisions, so creator ads (CGC) were a key focus for driving consideration and conversion. The format builds trust.

  5. Brand values at scale

    Tonies® is a legacy brand with real equity in the market. It was essential that our assets felt like Tonies® ads, because that is what captures a higher conversion rate.

Creator ads (CGC). Parents talking to parents: unboxings, group-chat recommendations, the words a child learnt this week.Meta · TikTok
The solution

100 assets in month one, 300 in six

In the first month alone we produced over 100 assets for Tonies®, across all three key channels. Over the course of six months, we produced over 300 pieces of unique ad creative, enabling a significant amplification in ad spend.

Our creative quickly shone through from a performance perspective too, driving an uptick in outbound CTR and conversion rate, which ultimately led to a reduction in CPA and enabled scalability.

The results

2.5x the profitable spend, 80% more purchases on Meta

As a result of our work together, Tonies® were able to increase year-on-year ad spend by 2.5x and saw a huge increase in scalability. Their purchases driven through acquisition also increased by 80% year on year on Meta alone.

The consideration we built through Q3 and into Q4 drove more aggressive acquisition across Q4. The full-funnel approach, asset variety and volume were all attributed to the result.
